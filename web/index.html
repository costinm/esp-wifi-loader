esp-wifi-loader

Installs a small Wi-Fi loader on a blank ESP32 board. The loader then receives your signed application over Wi-Fi: no USB cable needed after this step.

ESP Web Tools

Chrome or Edge, board connected over USB. 4 MB and 8 MB flash have different partition tables.

After flashing

A fresh board has no application, so it starts the loader. The loader joins the open DIRECT-xy-flash network if a flasher hosts it, otherwise it hosts ESP-RESCUE and announces state=blank.
