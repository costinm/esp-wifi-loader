esp-wifi-loader

Installs a small Wi-Fi loader on a blank ESP32, ESP32-S3 or ESP32-C6 board. The loader then receives your signed application over Wi-Fi: no USB cable needed after this step.

After flashing

A fresh board has no application yet, so it starts the loader and waits for a flasher. Use the Android app (Android 13 or newer; see the project page for the download):

  1. Tick Rescuer while the phone is charging, or tap Host flash network. The phone creates a Wi-Fi Direct network named DIRECT-ef-flash and the board joins it within about 15 seconds.
  2. If your phone cannot host it, tap Join ESP-RESCUE instead: with no flash network around, the board hosts ESP-RESCUE itself, and Android asks you once to confirm joining.
  3. The board appears in the list. Tap it: the app takes the image from a file you pick, from the domain the board reports, or, for a brand-new board, from the domain you type in, checks its signature and installs it.

Both networks use WPA2 with the public passphrase untrusted-open-mode: the board never needs your home Wi-Fi, and what it installs is protected by the image signature, not by the network. The loader stays on the board, so later upgrades need no cable.
